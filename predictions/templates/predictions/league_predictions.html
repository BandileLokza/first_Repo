📌 How Points Work

For every match, you predict three things: the result after 90 minutes, Team A goals and Team B goals after 90 minutes. Extra time and penalty shootouts are not included.

  • 5 points for correctly predicting which team wins, or if the match ends in a draw
  • 5 points for correctly predicting Team A goals
  • 5 points for correctly predicting Team B goals
  • Extra 5 bonus points if all your predictions are correct
Perfect prediction = 20 points
5 for correct result + 5 for Team A goals + 5 for Team B goals + 5 bonus points.

Make your predictions early, because once a match starts, predictions for that match are locked.
